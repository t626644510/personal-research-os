## Definition

In the selected paper, a harmonic cavity is the roughly 1500.36 MHz third-harmonic cavity used with the roughly 500.12 MHz main RF system in a bunch-lengthening design. A general reusable definition is UNRESOLVED.

## Engineering View

The paper reports one harmonic-cavity design with unloaded Q, Q0 = 36,000, and paper-reported R/Q = 68 ohm under a convention that cannot be generalized unconditionally; it also reports shunt impedance 2.45 Mohm and 90 mm effective length. It also discusses six cavities, passive and possible active operation, tuners, and couplers. Transfer to the current machine, port configuration, and lifetime meaning are UNRESOLVED.
