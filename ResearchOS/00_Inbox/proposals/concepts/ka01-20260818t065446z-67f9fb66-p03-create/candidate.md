# Frequency tuner

## Hover Summary

The selected paper reports copper rods and plungers used to change cavity resonance frequency; the mechanisms and ranges are design-specific and broader scope remains unresolved.

## Definition

In the selected paper, a frequency tuner is represented by copper rods in the main cavity and a plunger in the harmonic cavity, with reported geometry adjustments and frequency ranges. A general reusable definition is UNRESOLVED.

## Engineering View

The source describes two copper rods, each 95 mm in diameter; each moves ±50 mm, with about ±0.5 MHz range. It also describes two plungers, each 30 mm in diameter; each moves ±25 mm, with about ±0.5 MHz range. These values are design-specific.
